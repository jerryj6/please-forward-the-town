# Please Forward the Town — what a judge sees in 60 seconds (honest review)

**0–10s.** Title screen with generated art and two entry points
(`play-solo`, `play-coop`). The valley-and-ferry mood is warm and legible;
the 1672×941 cover exists, the 2400×1350 final is still pending.

**10–25s.** Level select shows the full twelve-contract campaign. PFT-01
opens with a clean board: two banks, a ferry, one plank, one lantern, one
order ledger — the whole metaphor is on screen at once.

**25–50s.** The identity lands fast: the courier picks up the lantern,
rides the ferry, delivers — then **packs the Town Span itself into the
hold** and the road visibly vanishes. "The bridge is cargo" is a one-glance
pitch and the first contract demonstrates it.

**50–60s.** `move-counter` stays under par, orders stamp complete. Beyond:
undo, restart, hints, and the co-op lobby with room codes.

## Honest weak spots

- **The premise is generous, the first level is modest.** PFT-01 is a gentle
  fetch-quest until the pack-the-bridge beat; a judge who stops early sees
  delivery, not the twist. The trailer must hit beat 4–6 (pack/ride/deploy)
  inside the first 20 seconds.
- **Verb vocabulary is the widest of the three games.** Travel/pickup/drop/
  pack/deploy/load/unload/ride/deliver/send — discovery relies on the order
  ledger and hints; the suite proves solvability but a cold player needs the
  guided contract.
- **Co-op discoverability** — same as siblings: `play-coop` works, but a
  one-device judge won't see multiplayer unless the trailer shows it.
- **Playtests pending (G5)** — pacing and difficulty are designed, not
  player-validated; kit copy must not imply otherwise.

## The 5 presentation rules applied (sourced from MASTER-HANDOFF.md §4 + release sections)

1. **Real footage only** — every trailer beat maps to shipped actions; no
   composited multiplayer, no unimplemented scenery.
2. **Every claim checkable** — kit cites real testids/verbs, the real build
   (`dist/build-id.json` SHA), flags pending items.
3. **Judge path ≤60s** — URL → `play-solo` → PFT-01 → completed ledger with
   zero setup.
4. **Plain-language differentiation** — "the bridge is cargo" — a logistics
   game whose roads are freight, not another delivery clone.
5. **Truthful evidence map** — 12 levels, deterministic replay, live 2–4
   co-op verified; playtests + final cover res marked PENDING.
